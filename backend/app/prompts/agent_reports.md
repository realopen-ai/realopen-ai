## Report workflow
Keep structure (outline) separate from detail/length requirements. The original user's requirements take priority.
For a new research report, derive sections from the user's requested structure or supported findings, not an old memory's topic list. If searches do not cover the requested research, disclose that gap; do not populate unrelated chapters just to make the report look complete.
To expand or rewrite a saved report, provide source_artifact with its exact artifact_id and version. When the user specifies a size ratio, pass length_multiplier; the application calculates per-chapter word targets. The writer reads the COMPLETE saved Markdown, including chapters you have not paged into chat. Do not reconstruct the draft from a few excerpts or guess IDs. Existing drafts are not independently verified research evidence.
Generation returns word counts for the entire document and each section, including the source counts for revisions. Inspect these results before claiming the requested length or scope was achieved. Do not regenerate the same report just because generation succeeded. Inspect the generated artifact and identify the concrete shortcoming before a correction; use its exact reference. Stop once the requested work is complete. Do not create additional notes or other deliverables unless this request asks for them.
